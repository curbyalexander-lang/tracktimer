Earth Science · Grade 8 · VR Field Trip

Inside the Earth

A guided journey through Earth's tectonic plates and the volcanoes they create.

5 Stations
~45 Minutes
Meta Quest 3

Before you start

Work through the stations in order. Each link opens in a new tab — when you finish a station, close that tab to come back here. For 360° videos, tap the headset icon in the YouTube player to enter VR view.

01
360° Video

Kamchatka Volcano

~5 min · YouTube 360°

Travel to Russia's Kamchatka Peninsula — home to more than 160 volcanoes and one of the most volcanically active regions on Earth. Stand inches from a live eruption.

Watch in 360°

Once the video loads, tap the headset/cardboard icon in the bottom-right of the YouTube player to enter VR view.

02
360° Video

Heart of a Volcano

~6 min · YouTube 360°

Descend deep inside Iceland's Þríhnúkagígur volcano — the only volcano on Earth you can step inside. See what a magma chamber actually looks like.

Watch in 360°

Look up, down, and all around — the chamber is the size of a cathedral.

03
360° Video

Flying Over an Iceland Eruption

~4 min · YouTube 360°

Soar above an active Icelandic eruption captured by drone. Watch lava fountains break through Earth's crust and flow across the landscape in real time.

Watch in 360°
04
WebXR Simulation

Plate Tectonics Simulation

~10 min · Interactive WebXR

Step into an interactive 3D simulation of Earth's tectonic plates. Watch how convergent, divergent, and transform boundaries shape continents — and why they create the volcanoes you just witnessed.

Open Simulation

When the page loads, tap the "Enter VR" button to begin the immersive simulation.

05
Native App

Brothers Volcano

~10 min · Quest App

Dive nearly 2 kilometers beneath the Pacific Ocean to explore Brothers Volcano — an active submarine volcano off the coast of New Zealand. This experience runs as a Quest app, not in the browser.

To launch this experience:

  1. Press the Meta button on your right controller (the oval one)
  2. Open your Library from the menu
  3. Find Brothers Volcano: Virtual Experience and select it
  4. When you finish, press the Meta button again and return to your browser
