How it reads light
The phone's camera is already a light meter — it just keeps its answer to itself. This app reads the exposure the camera chose (shutter time and sensor ISO), measures how bright the resulting frame actually is, and works backwards to the luminance in front of the lens.
  1. Frame pixels are converted out of sRGB back to linear light, then weighted to luma.
  2. Distance from 18% grey becomes a stop correction.
  3. N² ÷ t, adjusted to ISO 100, gives the camera's own exposure value.
  4. The two combine into scene EV, then N² / t = 2^EV gives your pair.
